Vyzora Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Vyzora is a data visualization app for iPhone and iPad. This Privacy Policy explains how Vyzora handles information when you use the app.

Summary

Vyzora is designed to be local-first.

  • Vyzora does not require an account.
  • Vyzora does not sell personal information.
  • Vyzora does not use advertising SDKs.
  • Vyzora does not use AI services to process your data.
  • Your datasets, charts, and reports are stored locally on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Information You Provide In The App

You may enter, import, or create data in Vyzora, including dataset names, table rows and columns, CSV file contents, chart titles and settings, saved chart views, report titles, and report contents.

This information is stored locally on your device. Vyzora does not upload this information to a Vyzora server.

CSV Import And File Access

If you import a CSV file, Vyzora reads the file you select so it can create or update a local dataset. The imported data remains local to your device unless you export or share it.

Exporting And Sharing

Vyzora may let you export or share chart images, PDF charts, PDF reports, and CSV datasets.

When you use the iOS share sheet, Files app, Photos, printing, email, messaging apps, cloud storage apps, or other third-party services, your exported content is handled by those services according to their own privacy policies.

Purchases And Subscriptions

Vyzora may offer paid features through Apple In-App Purchase, such as a Vyzora Pro subscription or lifetime unlock.

Purchases are processed by Apple. Vyzora may receive subscription status information from Apple so the app can unlock paid features, restore purchases, and manage access. Payment information, such as your card number, is handled by Apple and is not provided to Vyzora.

Analytics, Advertising, And Tracking

Vyzora does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

Vyzora does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

If analytics or crash reporting are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what is collected and why.

AI Processing

Vyzora does not send your datasets, charts, CSV files, or reports to AI services.

Children's Privacy

Vyzora is not directed to children under 13. Vyzora does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention

Data created in Vyzora remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or remove it through app features. Exported files remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Data Security

Vyzora relies on the security features of iOS and iPadOS to help protect local app data. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and any files you export or share.

International Users

Because Vyzora stores app data locally and does not operate a Vyzora server for user datasets, your app data generally remains on your device unless you choose to export or share it through another service.

Changes To This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Vyzora changes. When the policy changes, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated.

Contact
